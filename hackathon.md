About
Call My Agent Hackathon

AgentPhone (P26) is hosting Call My Agent Hackathon at Y Combinator in San Francisco on May 17, 2026.

First place gets a guaranteed YC interview. Plus cash, sponsor credits, phones for the winners, and bonus prizes across multiple tracks. Every attendee walks out with swag.

For years, AI agents were stuck in chat windows. That's changed. Voice models are cheap and fast, infrastructure for SMS, iMessage, email, and browsing has matured, and agents can finally do more than respond to a prompt. The phone book is open. Inboxes are fair game. The web is a playground.

TLDR: agents can now act in the real world. The only question left is what you'll build. So pick up the phone and call your agent.

What to Build:

Cold callers that close – voice agents that pitch, qualify, and book meetings
Inbox warriors – email agents that triage, reply, and follow up before you wake up
The negotiator – agents that haggle, book, and buy on your behalf
Group chat chaos – SMS and iMessage agents that text humans and other agents
Web wranglers – agents that navigate sites, fill forms, and get past logins
The fixer – multi-channel agents that combine voice, SMS, email, and browsing into one workflow
The doer – agents that book appointments, place orders, schedule deliveries, and get real things done in the physical world
Wildcard – anything where an agent touches the real world in a way we haven't seen yet
Who Should Apply:

Builders who want their agents to actually do something. If you're tired of demos that only work in a sandbox and you want to ship something a human could pay for tomorrow, this is for you. No prior idea required.

Why Join:

Build the kind of agents people will actually use, not just retweet
Compete with builders who care more about shipping than slides
Get hands-on with the infrastructure powering the next wave of agents
Walk away with something real, plus prizes, swag, and a shot at YC
